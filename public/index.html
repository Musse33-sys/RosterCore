How It Works
Step 1
Get an Account

Contact musse_33 on Discord to request a department account. Each department gets their own private roster.

Step 2
Build Your Roster

Log in and go to the Editor tab. Set up your rank groups, callsign ranges, positions, subdivisions and custom tags.

Step 3
Add Your Staff

Go to Share & Access to create staff accounts. Your staff log in with Member Login and can help manage the roster.

Step 4
Share Publicly

Click Share and copy the public link. Post it in your Discord so your whole community can view the roster anytime.

FAQ
How do I get access?

Contact musse_33 on Discord. Only approved departments get an account — there is no public registration.

Can my staff edit the roster?

Yes. Go to Share & Access, create a staff account for each person. They log in with Member Login and get full edit access to your roster only.

What if someone misuses access?

Go to Share & Access and click Remove next to their name. Their account is deleted instantly and they can no longer log in.

Is the roster public?

The roster is read-only public — anyone with the public link can view it. Only logged-in staff can make changes.

Does data save automatically?

Yes. Every change saves to the cloud automatically within seconds. Your data is never lost on refresh.

What are callsign ranges?

In the Editor, each rank group has a From and To callsign (e.g. 1W-01 to 1W-06). New members get auto-assigned the lowest available slot in their group.
